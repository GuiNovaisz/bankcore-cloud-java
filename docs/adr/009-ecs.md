# ADR-009: ECS Fargate compute

Status: accepted for foundation. Date: 2026-11-21.

Choose independent small Fargate tasks to minimize host/fleet operations. Managed Instances offers EC2 hardware flexibility with managed infrastructure, while EC2 adds fleet ownership. Neither has evidence-based benefit for this workload; revisiting sustained load/cost is allowed. No Kubernetes.

Consequences: implement/test the chosen boundary and retain the documented limitation; re-evaluate with measured workload or an explicit production requirement rather than adding technology for its name.
